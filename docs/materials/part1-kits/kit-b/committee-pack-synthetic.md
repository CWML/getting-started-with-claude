# Preclinical Curriculum Committee: charter and October meeting notes

> Fictional/synthetic CWML class file. Contains no real people or patient data.

## Part 1. Charter

**Purpose.** The Preclinical Curriculum Committee oversees the Year 1 and Year 2 courses of the medical school curriculum and makes sure they meet the program's learning objectives.

**Duties.** The committee:

1. Reviews and approves course objectives and major changes to Year 1 and Year 2 courses.
2. Reviews course evaluations and student feedback each block, and asks for follow-up when needed.
3. Sets and reviews grading and assessment policies for Year 1 and Year 2.
4. Coordinates the schedule across course blocks (exams, review sessions, holidays).
5. Reports to the Education Committee once each term.

**Membership (by role).**

- Voting (7): Chair (a faculty member); four Course Directors (Blocks 1–4); two Student Representatives (Year 1 and Year 2).
- Non-voting: Associate Dean for Curriculum; Curriculum Office Coordinator (takes notes); Library Liaison.

**Quorum.** A majority of voting members (4 of 7) must be present to vote.

**Meetings.** Monthly, on the third Thursday, for 60 minutes.

**Action items.** The Curriculum Office Coordinator sends the summary and action items to all members within 3 business days of each meeting. Every action item needs an owner (by role) and a due date.

**Confidentiality.** Individual student matters (appeals, grades, accommodations, professionalism concerns) are discussed in closed session. They are not recorded in the general notes and are never included in committee emails. Student Representatives leave the room for these items.
