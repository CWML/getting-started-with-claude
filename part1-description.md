# Getting Started with Claude

## Title

Getting Started with Claude: Build a Project You'll Keep Using

## Modality & location

In person: SHM L 111

## Duration

60 minutes

## Brief description of training

A hands-on workshop in the Claude app for anyone at the School of Medicine. No programming experience needed. You'll build a Claude Project (a workspace with its own instructions and files) and leave with a tool you can reuse. Before class, choose a practice kit: the Article Explainer, which turns a research article into a plain-language explainer, or the Committee Assistant for administrative staff, which turns meeting notes into minutes and action items. In class you'll decide which Claude account and settings fit your data, prompt your Project for a real task, and check its answers, quotes, and citations before you use them. You'll also see how to adjust the model and effort and get a short look at skills and Cowork. Opening your Project in the Claude phone app is optional. Every file is public or made up, and no patient data is used. Bring a laptop and sign in to your Claude account before class. No working account? You can pair with a neighbor in class.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this class, you'll be able to:

1. Identify the right tool and settings for your data.
2. Apply prompting patterns to a real task.
3. Evaluate Claude's answers and citations before you use them.
4. Create a Project you can reuse for your own work.
